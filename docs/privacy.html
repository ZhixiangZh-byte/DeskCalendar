Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-24 · DeskCalendar home

Summary

DeskCalendar is a desktop application that runs on your own computer. It connects directly to Google Calendar and Google Tasks using your Google account. There is no DeskCalendar server, and the developer does not collect, receive, store, sell or share any of your data.

Google user data the app accesses

  • calendar.calendarlist.readonly: the list of calendars you have selected, to know which calendars to display.
  • calendar.events: read events to display them; create, edit or delete events only when you do so in the app.
  • tasks: read tasks to display them; create, edit, complete or delete tasks only when you do so in the app.

How the data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to show your calendar and tasks in the widgets and to apply the changes you make.
  • A cache of recently displayed events and tasks, and a queue of changes made while offline, are stored locally on your computer (WebView2 local storage) so that the widgets keep working offline.
  • The OAuth refresh token is stored in Windows Credential Manager on your computer. Access tokens are kept only in memory.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking. No data is transmitted anywhere except to Google's APIs.

Limited Use disclosure

DeskCalendar's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

  • Use "Sign out of Google" in the app (settings or tray menu): the refresh token is revoked and removed, and local caches are cleared.
  • You can also revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Uninstalling the app removes the program; local caches live in your Windows user profile.
