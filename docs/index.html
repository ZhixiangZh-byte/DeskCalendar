DeskCalendar

Transparent desktop calendar widgets for Windows, backed by your Google Calendar and Google Tasks.
贴在 Windows 桌面上的透明日历组件，直接读写你的 Google Calendar 和 Google Tasks。

Privacy Policy 隐私政策 Terms of Service 服务条款 Source code 源代码

What it does

  • Month, day, Eisenhower-matrix (important × urgent), review and long-term-plan widgets that stay on the Windows desktop.
  • Create, edit, complete and delete Google Tasks; create and edit events on your own Google calendars.
  • Works offline: changes are queued locally and synced when the network returns.
  • Optional Chinese lunar dates and traditional festivals.

功能

  • 月历、日视图、四象限、回顾箱、长期规划，每个都是可以贴在桌面上的组件。
  • 新建、编辑、勾选、删除 Google Tasks 任务；新建和编辑自己日历上的 Google Calendar 日程。
  • 离线可用：断网时的修改保存在本地，联网后自动同步。
  • 可选显示农历和传统节日。

How your Google data is used

DeskCalendar runs entirely on your computer and talks directly to Google's APIs. There is no DeskCalendar server; the developer never receives your data. See the Privacy Policy for details.
